# WORLD BIBLE: NEON NOIR - THE SILICON PULSE

## SETTING: NEO-VERIDIA (Year 2084)
Neo-Veridia is a hyper-dense megacity built on the ruins of old Seattle. The atmosphere is thick with "Digital Smog"—a luminous, floating particulate matter from discarded nanobots that refracts neon light into oily, chromatic patterns.

## VISUAL AESTHETIC (The 'Look')
- **Lighting:** High-contrast Chiaroscuro. Primary light sources are flickering pink/cyan neon signs and the warm, amber glow of synthetic street food stalls.
- **Color Palette:** 
    - #0A0E14 (Deep Obsidian - Shadows)
    - #FF00FF (Electric Magenta - Neon Accents)
    - #00FFFF (Cyber Cyan - Data Streams)
    - #FFBF00 (Amber Synthetic - Low-light warmth)
- **Textures:** Wet asphalt, brushed chrome, holographic glitches, and heavy rain.

## KEY CHARACTER: ELARA VANCE
- **Role:** A 'Memory Scavenger'—someone who retrieves deleted data from discarded neural implants.
- **Physical Appearance:** 
    - Hair: Short, asymmetrical undercut, dyed iridescent silver.
    - Eyes: Cybernetic replacements with a subtle gold iris ring.
    - Clothing: A heavy, weathered tech-wear trench coat in matte charcoal, featuring integrated glowing blue fiber-optics along the seams.
- **Signature Item:** A vintage brass-cased data reader that contrasts with her high-tech environment.

## CORE CONFLICT
The 'Great Wipe'—a massive data corruption event that is slowly erasing the digital memories of the city's inhabitants, causing a crisis of identity and societal collapse.
