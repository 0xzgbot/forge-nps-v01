The company

Forge NPS is not another prompt wrapper. It is a virtual production agency with memory.

Most AI creative workflows lose context between brief, plan, prompt, render, audit, and retry. Forge closes the loop. Every shot carries its rationale, skills used, model choices, critique, audit result, and remediation history.
