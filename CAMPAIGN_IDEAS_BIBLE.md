## 🎬 TV COMMERCIALS & SPOTS

### 1. "The Last Barista"
**Format:** 30s Cinematic Spot  
**Genre:** Sci-Fi / Dystopian  
**Visual Style:** Blade Runner 2049 meets cozy coffee shop. Neon rain, steam, warm tungsten interiors.  
**Brief:** In a world where every job is automated, one human barista still works. A lonely android orders a latte. The human smiles. The android's LED eyes flicker — is that emotion?  
**Shots:** 8 (ext. cafe rainy night, int. warm glow, android POV, latte art macro, eye close-up, smile, pull back to reveal empty street, logo)

### 2. "Gravity Forgot Her"
**Format:** 15s Luxury Fashion  
**Genre:** Surrealism  
**Visual Style:** High-speed phantom flex, fabric suspended mid-air, zero-gravity debris, golden hour.  
**Brief:** A woman in a flowing red dress walks through a room where gravity is failing. Furniture floats, water hangs in spheres, her dress defies physics. Tagline: "Weightless elegance."

### 3. "The Unboxing"
**Format:** 30s Tech Product  
**Genre:** ASMR / Hypnotic  
**Visual Style:** Macro cinematography, perfect white studio, satisfying peel sounds visualized.  
**Brief:** A product box opens in extreme slow motion. Light spills out. Materials reveal themselves — brushed aluminum, etched glass, woven cable. Each texture given hero treatment.

### 4. "Midnight Diner — Shift Change"
**Format:** 30s Food & Bev  
**Genre:** Neo-Noir  
**Visual Style:** Edward Hopper meets Tokyo cyberpunk. Wet streets, neon kanji, steam from grills.  
**Brief:** A chef passes his spatula to the night shift robot. They share a knowing look. Robot produces perfect ramen. Chef nods approval. "Some things don't need sleep."

### 5. "Paper Planes"
**Format:** 60s Emotional Narrative  
**Genre:** Drama  
**Visual Style:** Naturalistic, warm film grain, shallow depth of field, golden hour.  
**Brief:** An old man folds paper planes and throws them off his balcony. A young girl in the building across catches them. Montage of their wordless friendship growing through folded paper messages.

### 6. "The Factory of Dreams"
**Format:** 30s Automotive  
**Genre:** Industrial Poetic  
**Visual Style:** Monumental scale, sparks, robot arms as dancers, dramatic shadows, single car emerging.  
**Brief:** Car manufacturing reimagined as a ballet. Robot arms move with grace. Sparks become fireworks. The finished vehicle rolls out into sunlight. No voiceover needed.

### 7. "Echoes"
**Format:** 15s Audio Brand  
**Genre:** Abstract / Sound-Forward  
**Visual Style:** Cymatics — sound waves visualized in liquids, powders, and light.  
**Brief:** A single note plays. Visualize its journey through different media — water ripples, sand patterns, laser refraction, flame distortion. Logo formed by the final wave pattern.

### 8. "First Contact"
**Format:** 30s Telecom / Connectivity  
**Genre:** Sci-Fi / Hopeful  
**Visual Style:** Vast landscapes, satellite dishes, star trails, a single house lit in the wilderness.  
**Brief:** A child in a remote cabin points a flashlight at the sky. In orbit, a satellite turns. A signal travels through undersea cables, cell towers, fiber optics — connecting her to someone on the other side of the world.

### 9. "The Heist"
**Format:** 30s Banking / Security  
**Genre:** Thriller / Comedy  
**Visual Style:** Ocean's Eleven slick, split screens, security cam aesthetic, gold and vault green.  
**Brief:** A team of thieves breaks into a vault. Inside: not gold, but data. The vault is empty because the bank moved everything to the cloud. Thieves check their phones — accounts secure. "Sorry guys, wrong decade."

### 10. "Generations"
**Format:** 60s Heritage Brand  
**Genre:** Documentary-style  
**Visual Style:** Photo-real mixed with archival aesthetic, warm tones, time-lapse elements.  
**Brief:** A single object (watch/furniture/tool) passes through generations — 1920s craftsman, 1960s mother, 1990s teenager, today. Each era's aesthetic faithfully recreated. Object ages beautifully.

### 11. "The Speed of Thought"
**Format:** 15s AI / Tech  
**Genre:** Abstract / Data Visualization  
**Visual Style:** Neural network visualization, light speed tunnels, brain scans, clean UI overlays.  
**Brief:** A question appears. Synapses fire. Data rivers converge. An answer crystallizes. All in the time it takes to blink.

### 12. "Under the Same Sky"
**Format:** 30s Travel / Airline  
**Genre:** Emotional / Aspirational  
**Visual Style:** Aerial cinematography, cotton clouds, cities as jewels at night, connecting flight paths as gold threads.  
**Brief:** Parallel lives in different cities — a musician in Tokyo, a grandmother in Paris, a student in Nairobi — all looking at the same moon. A plane connects their stories.
