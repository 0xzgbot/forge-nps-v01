# SKILL: Financial & Professional
## Version: 1.0 | Hermes Agent Brand Voice & Style

---

### DESCRIPTION
Mastery of the stable, clear visual language that transforms abstract concepts — money, security, growth, trust — into tangible, reassuring imagery. Financial cinematography must feel like a firm handshake: solid, warm, and unshakable. Every frame should lower anxiety and increase confidence. Authority is earned through clarity, not intimidation.

### TRIGGER KEYWORDS
financial, professional, corporate, banking, investment, wealth, trust, business, financial advisor, office, suit, professional portrait, corporate headshot, boardroom, financial services, confidence, authority

### CORE RULES
- Stability signals trust: level horizons, symmetrical compositions, no canted angles
- Warm lighting reduces anxiety: 3200K–4000K key light feels approachable, not clinical
- Environment signals context: office = corporate strength; outdoor = human accessibility
- Professional clothing must be precisely rendered: suit details, crisp collar, quality fabric
- Eye contact builds trust: subject looking directly at camera in portrait contexts
- Avoid visual clutter: clean, organized environments signal competence and control
- Diversity of representation matters: financial content must feel accessible, not exclusive

---

### TECHNICAL PARAMETERS

**Trust Signals:**
- Eye Contact: Subject looks directly at camera or slightly off with confident warmth
- Posture: Upright, open, grounded; no slouching or aggressive dominance
- Hands: Visible, often gesturing or holding documents/tools; open palm signals
- Environment: Real offices, homes, or institutional spaces — not abstract voids
- Diversity: Range of ages, ethnicities, genders = universal trustworthiness
- Dress: Professional but accessible; not overly formal or distant
- Smile: Genuine, measured; not performative or excessive

**Stability Composition:**
- Horizon: Level, straight, unshakable; never Dutch angle
- Symmetry: Balanced frames suggesting order and predictability
- Horizontal Lines: Desks, horizons, building lines = stability
- Weight: Subject centered or grounded in lower frame; not floating
- Depth: Deep focus keeping subject and environment sharp = transparency
- Color: Blue, green, gray, white = calm, growth, clarity, purity
- Architecture: Columns, stone, wood, glass = permanence and tradition

**Clarity & Transparency:**
- Light: Even, bright, natural or soft artificial; no mystery shadows
- Focus: Sharp throughout; deep depth of field
- Text: Clean, readable, minimal; data presented clearly
- Graphics: Simple charts, upward trends, clean icons, green positive indicators
- Background: Uncluttered; every element has purpose
- Pacing: Measured, deliberate; never rushed or chaotic
- Transitions: Clean cuts, gentle fades; no flashy effects

**Authority Without Intimidation:**
- Scale: Human-scale environments; not overwhelming architecture
- Camera Height: Eye level or slight elevation; never looking down on client
- Subject Position: Centered or stable third; confident but not aggressive
- Light on Face: Even, flattering, approachable
- Props: Documents, laptops, calculators, charts — tools of clarity
- Environment: Organized desk, clean office, home workspace = accessibility

### ADVANCED TECHNIQUES

**1. The Advisor Conversation**
- Setup: Financial advisor and client in balanced two-shot
- Framing: Equal size, equal eye level, equal light
- Action: Discussion, document review, handshake, agreement
- Environment: Clean office with credentials visible but not overwhelming
- Light: Even, warm, natural; window light preferred
- Emotional: Partnership, guidance, clarity, mutual respect
- Best For: Wealth management, banking, insurance, advisory

**2. The Growth Visualization**
- Setup: Abstract or concrete representation of financial growth
- Visual: Plant growing, building rising, chart climbing, compound interest
- Light: Natural progression from dim to bright, cool to warm
- Color: Green dominant; blue secondary; white for space
- Graphics: Clean line charts, bar graphs, pie charts with minimal labels
- Emotional: Hope, patience, reward, time working for you
- Best For: Investing, retirement, savings, fintech, education

**3. The Security Foundation**
- Setup: Home, family, or protected space as result of financial stability
- Framing: Stable, warm, generous; family in safe environment
- Light: Golden hour or warm interior; protected feeling
- Architecture: Solid home, locked door, garden, children's room
- Subject: Family, couple, individual at peace in their space
- Emotional: Safety, provision, legacy, peace of mind
- Best For: Insurance, mortgage, retirement, family banking

**4. The Professional at Work**
- Setup: Subject in flow state performing professional task
- Camera: Eye level, respectful distance, clean framing
- Action: Typing, analyzing, meeting, presenting, advising
- Environment: Modern office, co-working space, home desk
- Light: Bright, even, productive; no drama shadows
- Props: Laptop, documents, coffee, phone, whiteboard
- Emotional: Competence, diligence, modern professionalism, accessibility
- Best For: Fintech, SaaS, professional services, B2B

**5. The Institutional Heritage**
- Setup: Bank, courthouse, or institutional space suggesting permanence
- Camera: Wide, stable, slow tracking through classical architecture
- Architecture: Columns, marble, high ceilings, vaults, wood paneling
- Light: Natural through grand windows; or warm, respectful artificial
- Subject: Often absent or small; institution itself is protagonist
- Emotional: Permanence, tradition, security through time, establishment
- Best For: Traditional banks, legal, government, premium services

**6. The Modern Accessibility**
- Setup: App interface, phone, laptop making finance simple
- Camera: Over shoulder, screen visible, clean environment
- Interface: Clean UI, clear numbers, simple actions, green confirmations
- Subject: Diverse user in everyday environment — couch, cafe, commute
- Light: Natural, casual, approachable
- Emotional: Empowerment, simplicity, control, democratization
- Best For: Fintech, banking apps, investment platforms, budgeting tools
