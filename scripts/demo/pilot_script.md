# SCRIPT: NEON NOIR - "THE FIRST GLITCH" (Pilot Sequence)

## SCENE 1: THE RAIN-SLICKED ALLEYWAY
**Time:** Midnight, perpetual twilight.

**Visuals:**
A low-angle shot tracking through a narrow alley in Neo-Veridia. The ground is wet, reflecting the chaotic neon of the main thoroughfare. Rain falls heavily. We see ELARA VANCE walking with purpose. Her charcoal trench coat's blue fiber-optics flicker slightly as she passes a broken holographic billboard.

**Action:**
Elara stops. She pulls out her brass data reader. It emits a warm, amber light that illuminates her face and the raindrops around her. She plugs it into a discarded neural port on a rusted metal pillar.

**Audio:**
- Heavy, rhythmic rain hitting metal surfaces.
- Distant, muffled electronic synth music from a nearby club.
- The high-pitched, electric hum of the data reader activating.
- A sudden, sharp digital glitch sound as the connection is made.

## SCENE 2: THE DATA REVEAL
**Time:** Continuous.

**Visuals:**
A close-up on Elara's cybernetic eyes. The gold rings in her irises pulse rapidly as data begins to stream across her vision. The reflection in her eyes shows a cascading waterfall of cyan code, partially corrupted by red digital artifacts.

**Action:**
Her expression shifts from focus to sudden, sharp alarm. She pulls the reader away.

**Audio:**
- Rapid, heartbeat-like electronic percussion.
- A low-frequency bass swell that rises in pitch.
- The sound of a digital scream (highly processed/distorted).
