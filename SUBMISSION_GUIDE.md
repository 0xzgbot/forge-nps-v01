## 🎯 What Judges Actually Want

The judging criteria are **creativity, usefulness, and presentation.** Here's how to nail all three:

### Creativity — "Show us something we haven't seen"

Most submissions will show:
- ❌ "Our agent calls an API"
- ❌ "Our agent remembers things"
- ❌ "We generated some images"

**You will show:**
- ✅ **A complete creative campaign** born from a single brief
- ✅ **An agent that learns style DNA** and applies it across video, image, and social formats
- ✅ **Cross-format consistency** — the same brand identity in a TV commercial, an Instagram carousel, and a TikTok
- ✅ **Teach Mode** — the moment an AI learns a creative rule and never forgets it

### Usefulness — "Would a creative professional actually use this?"

Frame everything around real creative workflows:
- A social media manager who needs 30 posts/month, all on-brand
- An indie filmmaker who needs consistent characters across 50 shots
- A product photographer who needs 100 variations, same lighting
- A brand designer who needs campaigns that don't drift

**Forge solves all of them.** One pipeline. Any format. Perfect memory.

### Presentation — "Make us feel something in 90 seconds"

Your submission is a **Twitter video**. Judges will scroll past dozens. Yours needs to:
1. **Hook in 3 seconds** — beautiful output, not architecture diagrams
2. **Show the arc** — brief → campaign → consistency → learning
3. **End with a punch** — "This is Forge. The creative studio with a memory."

## 🎬 Demo Video Script (60 Seconds)

This is your actual submission. Every second counts.

### 0:00–0:05 — THE HOOK
**Visual:** Rapid montage — Instagram carousel, TikTok frame, TV commercial still, short film shot.
**Narration:** *"What if you could brief an AI like a creative director — and get back an entire campaign?"*
**Text overlay:** One brief · One agent · Every format

### 0:05–0:15 — THE PROBLEM
**Visual:** Split screen. Left: beautiful brand asset. Right: same brand, wrong colors, wrong vibe, wrong everything.
**Narration:** *"AI can generate one beautiful thing. But campaign 2? Shot 10? Every prompt is a guess. Your brand drifts. Your character forgets who they are."*
**Text overlay:** AI has no memory

### 0:15–0:25 — THE STACK
**Visual:** Clean cards animate in — Kimi 2.6, FLUX2 Dev, Z-Image Turbo, LTX 2.3, WAN 2.2.
**Narration:** *"Kimi 2.6 directs. FLUX2 renders quality. Z-Image iterates fast. LTX and WAN handle video. One pipeline. Any format."*
**Text overlay:** Kimi 2.6 · FLUX2 · Z-Image · LTX · WAN

### 0:25–0:40 — THE PROJECT
**Visual:** Dashboard. Cursor types a brief: *"Sienna Nomad — outdoor lifestyle brand, earth tones, adventure vibe."* Clicks **Generate Campaign**. A carousel of 12 Instagram posts generates. Then a 15-second TikTok. Then a TV commercial storyboard.
**Narration:** *"Feed Forge a brief. It writes the campaign. Generates every asset. And Hermes — the learning agent — makes sure every post, every frame, every shot stays perfectly consistent."*
**Text overlay:** 12 posts · 3 formats · 1 brand identity

### 0:40–0:50 — TEACH MODE
**Visual:** Teach Mode in the dashboard. Deliberately strip a brand color. Generate → fails. Consolidate → insight born. Re-run → auto-fixed → passes.
**Narration:** *"Watch this. We break the brand color. Hermes catches it, learns the fix, and never makes that mistake again."*
**Text overlay:** 🧠 New insight born · Confidence 94%

### 0:50–0:60 — THE CLOSE
**Visual:** Export Brain button clicked. JSON downloads. Fade to logo.
**Narration:** *"Export everything your campaign learned. A portable brain for your next project. This is Forge."*
**Text overlay:** Forge · The Creative Studio with a Memory
