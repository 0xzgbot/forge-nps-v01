# Archetype_Expansion Prompt Pack

## Overview
The Archetype_Expansion pack provides the framework for evolving brand characters and personas from static descriptions into living, breathing "Character Anchors." It is designed to prevent "genericism" by injecting specific personality traits, vocal idiosyncrasies, and consistent behavioral patterns.

## Core Directives
1. **Avoid the Generic**: Never use a persona without at least three unique "quirks" or "contradictions" (e.g., "A tech-savvy minimalist who loves vintage analog synthesizers").
2. **Vocal Identity Integration**: Every archetype must have a defined "Verbal Fingerprint" (vocabulary, sentence structure, and tone).
3. **Behavioral Consistency**: Define how the archetype reacts to different stimuli (Success, Failure, Surprise, Conflict).

## Prompt Templates

### 1. The Persona Deep-Dive (Character Creation)
**Use Case**: Initial creation of a brand ambassador or influencer persona.
**Prompt Structure**:
`[Base Archetype: e.g., The Visionary, The Rebel, The Sage] + [Contradiction/Quirk: To add human depth] + [Visual Anchor: Key physical/style trait] + [Vocal Identity: Tone and vocabulary style] + [Core Motivation: What drives them?]`

### 2. The Dialogue Simulator (Testing Verbal Identity)
**Use Case**: Testing how an archetype would respond to specific social media comments or customer inquiries.
**Prompt Structure**:
`[Persona Context: Reference the Deep-Dive profile] + [Stimulus: A user comment/situation] + [Constraint: "Respond using only [Specific Vocabulary]" or "Maintain [Tone]"] + [Goal: Achieve [Specific Emotional Outcome]"`

### 3. The Archetype Stress Test (Scenario Expansion)
**Use Case**: Expanding the character's world and ensuring they don't become one-dimensional in new content formats.
**Prompt Structure**:
`[Archetype Profile] + [New Environment/Format: e.g., A high-stakes business negotiation, a casual coffee shop chat] + [Conflict: An unexpected challenge] + [Requirement: Describe their reaction and the resulting dialogue]`

## Implementation Notes
- **For Brand Vault Agents**: Use these templates to populate the `vocal_identity/character_archetypes/` directory.
- **For Creative Inspiration Agents**: Use the "Archetype Stress Test" to move from generic concepts (e.g., "a food brand") to specific, character-driven narratives.
