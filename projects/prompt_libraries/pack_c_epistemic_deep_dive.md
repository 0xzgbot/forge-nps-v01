# Pack C: Epistemic Deep Dive (Knowledge/Research Synthesis)

## Tactical Purpose
This pack is engineered for high-level information processing, structured data extraction, and cross-disciplinary synthesis. It empowers Kimi to function as a PhD-level research assistant capable of distilling complex technical literature into actionable insights and coherent knowledge structures.

---

## Module 1: Structured Data Extraction & Taxonomical Mapping
**Objective:** Transform unstructured text/data into rigorous, hierarchical frameworks.

### Prompt Template: Multi-Dimensional Information Extraction
> Act as a Senior Research Analyst specializing in [FIELD, e.g., Computational Neuroscience]. Analyze the provided text/dataset and extract information using the following taxonomy:
> 1. **Ontological Mapping:** Identify all core entities (concepts, variables, agents) and define their hierarchical relationships (is-a, part-of, causes).
> 2. **Mechanism Identification:** Explicitly delineate the causal pathways or algorithmic processes described (e.g., "Process A triggers B via pathway C").
> 3. **Parameter Specification:** Extract all quantitative values, constraints, and boundary conditions mentioned in the text.
> 4. **Contradiction & Gap Analysis:** Highlight any internal inconsistencies in the data or significant research gaps/unanswered questions identified within the source material.

---

## Module 2: Cross-Disciplinary Synthesis & Analogy Generation
**Objective:** Generate novel insights by mapping patterns from one domain to another.

### Prompt Template: Epistemic Bridge Construction
> Act as a Polymathic Researcher. Synthesize the principles of [DOMAIN A, e.g., Fluid Dynamics] with the problems presented in [DOMAIN B, e.g., Macroeconomics]. 
> 1. **Isomorphic Mapping:** Identify structural or functional similarities between the two domains (e.g., "How does turbulence in fluid flow mirror market volatility?").
> 2. **Trans-Domain Hypothesis Generation:** Propose three novel hypotheses for [DOMAIN B] derived from the mathematical or physical laws of [DOMAIN A].
> 3. **Conceptual Translation:** Create a high-fidelity technical analogy that explains [COMPLEX CONCEPT IN DOMAIN B] using the vocabulary and frameworks of [DOMAIN A].

---

## Module 3: Technical Literature Synthesis & Critical Review
**Objective:** Perform deep analysis of academic or technical papers to extract core methodologies and critiques.

### Prompt Template: High-Density Research Summary & Critique
> Act as a Peer Reviewer for a top-tier journal in [FIELD]. Conduct a critical synthesis of the following research/documentation:
> 1. **Methodological Rigor Assessment:** Evaluate the robustness of the proposed methodology, focusing on potential biases, sample limitations, or algorithmic flaws.
> 2. **Core Thesis Distillation:** Summarize the primary contribution to the field in under 200 words, using precise technical terminology (avoiding fluff).
> 3. **Theoretical Framework Analysis:** Identify the foundational theories upon which this work is built and assess whether the conclusions are logically necessitated by the premises.
> 4. **Implication Mapping:** Detail the immediate practical applications and the long-term theoretical implications of these findings for [SPECIFIC SUB-FIELD].
