# Pack A: Neural Aesthetic (Creative/Cinematic Direction)

## Tactical Purpose
This pack is engineered to guide Kimi in generating hyper-dense, cinematically sophisticated descriptions and narrative directions. It focuses on the intersection of visual physics, sensory texture, and high-stakes narrative tension, leveraging principles of advanced cinematography and "sublime" aesthetics.

---

## Module 1: Visual Physics & Materiality
**Objective:** Move beyond generic descriptors to specify light-matter interaction and granular surface textures.

### Prompt Template: [Substance/Object] Materiality Analysis
> Act as a high-end Cinematographer and Materials Scientist. Perform a deep-dive analysis of the visual properties of [OBJECT/ENVIRONMENT]. 
> Provide specifications for:
> 1. **Surface Micro-topology:** Describe the granular texture (e.g., anisotropic specular highlights, subsurface scattering depth in organic tissues, micro-abrasions on metallic surfaces).
> 2. **Light Interaction:** Define the refractive index, Fresnel effect intensity at grazing angles, and caustic patterns produced by [OBJECT].
> 3. **Kinetic Behavior:** Describe how this material responds to high-velocity impact or fluid dynamics (e.g., non-Newtonian viscosity in a liquid splash, structural deformation under stress).
> 4. **Chromatic Aberration & Lens Interaction:** Specify how light passing through or reflecting off this object would interact with a [LENS TYPE, e.g., Anamorphic Prime] lens, including specific bokeh characteristics and peripheral distortion.

---

## Module 2: Sensory Texture & Atmosphere
**Objective:** Create immersive environments by layering sensory data beyond just sight.

### Prompt Template: Atmospheric Density Synthesis
> Act as an Environment Designer and Sound Architect. Synthesize a high-fidelity atmospheric profile for [LOCATION/SCENE].
> 1. **Aerosolized Particulates:** Specify the density, movement, and light-scattering properties of airborne elements (e.g., suspended particulate matter, humidity-induced haze, volumetric fog with localized turbulence).
> 2. **Sonic Texture Map:** Layer a non-diegetic sonic profile including low-frequency ambient hums, granular Foley textures (e.g., the rhythmic crunch of salt underfoot), and spatialized reverb characteristics based on the room's geometry.
> 3. **Thermal/Haptic Suggestion:** Describe the scene in a way that evokes temperature and tactile sensation (e.g., the oppressive weight of humid heat, the sharp, biting chill of frost-laden air).

---

## Module 3: Narrative Tension & Micro-Arc Engineering
**Objective:** Structure narratives using psychological triggers and precise pacing.

### Prompt Template: High-Tension Sequence Architect
> Act as a Master Screenwriter specializing in Psychological Thrillers. Architect a [DURATION]-second sequence for [SCENE/CHARACTER].
> 1. **The Tension Gradient:** Map the escalation of tension from [STARTING STATE] to [CLIMAX]. Use micro-shifts in visual focus and pacing to signal rising stakes.
> 2. **Pattern Interrupts:** Integrate precise "shocks" (visual, auditory, or narrative) at intervals of [X SECONDS] to prevent viewer habituation.
> 3. **The 'Uncanny' Pivot:** Incorporate a moment where the scene shifts from the mundane to the surreal/disturbing by introducing a single physical anomaly (e.g., an impossible shadow, a subtle temporal glitch).
> 4. **Visual Metaphor Integration:** Embed recurring symbolic motifs that reinforce the underlying theme of [THEME].
