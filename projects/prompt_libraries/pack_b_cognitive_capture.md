# Pack B: Cognitive Capture (Strategic Marketing/Viral Mechanics)

## Tactical Purpose
This pack is designed to weaponize psychological pattern interrupts and algorithmic engagement loops. It provides Kimi with the frameworks necessary to engineer content that maximizes dwell time, comment velocity, and social proofing within high-competition digital ecosystems (TikTok, X, Instagram).

---

## Module 1: Psychological Pattern Interrupts
**Objective:** Disrupt the "infinite scroll" through visual and cognitive dissonance.

### Prompt Template: The 'Sublime Valley' Hook Generator
> Act as a Behavioral Psychologist and Viral Content Strategist. Design three distinct "Hook Architectures" for [CONTENT TOPIC] intended to trigger immediate attention within 1.5 seconds of exposure.
> For each hook, specify:
> 1. **The Visual Paradox:** Describe an image or video frame that presents a logical contradiction (e.g., hyper-realistic physics applied to an impossible scenario, like liquid metal flowing uphill).
> 2. **Cognitive Dissonance Trigger:** Identify the specific mental mechanism being exploited (e.g., Violation of Expectancy, The Uncanny Valley, or Pattern Recognition Error).
> 3. **Arousal Vector:** Define whether the hook aims for "Awe," "Confusion," "Controversy," or "Disgust" to drive immediate dopamine/cortisol spikes.

---

## Module 2: Engagement Loop Engineering
**Objective:** Maximize algorithmic signals (comments, shares, saves) through structured interaction design.

### Prompt Template: Algorithmic Feedback Loop Architect
> Act as a Growth Hacker and Social Algorithm Specialist. Develop an engagement strategy for [CAMPAIGN/VIDEO] focused on maximizing the "Weighted Engagement Formula."
> 1. **Debate-Bait Construction:** Instead of asking questions, formulate three controversial or debatable statements related to [TOPIC] that force users into polarized comment sections.
> 2. **The 'Save' Incentive (Referenceable Value):** Design a piece of content within the sequence that is so information-dense or visually complex (e.g., an "impossible" tutorial or a dense data visualization) that it necessitates a "Save" for later reference.
> 3. **Comment Velocity Trigger:** Identify a specific, subtle error or "Easter Egg" to embed in the content to encourage users to point it out in the comments, thereby driving initial engagement velocity.

---

## Module 3: Narrative Micro-Arc Pacing
**Objective:** Optimize retention through precision pacing and tension management.

### Prompt Template: Retention-Optimized Sequence Blueprint
> Act as a Digital Storyteller specializing in short-form video (TikTok/Reels). Map out a [DURATION]-second narrative arc for [CONTENT SUBJECT].
> 1. **0-3s (The Hook):** The immediate visual/auditory disruption.
> 2. **3-8s (The Curiosity Gap):** Establishing the stakes or the "problem" that must be resolved.
> 3. **8-X s (Escalation & Pattern Interrupts):** A sequence of micro-payoffs with a pattern interrupt (visual shift, audio change) every [Y] seconds to maintain arousal.
> 4. **Final 5s (The Loop/CTA):** A seamless transition back to the start for rewatch loops, or a high-friction Call to Action (e.g., "Don't believe me? Check the link.")
